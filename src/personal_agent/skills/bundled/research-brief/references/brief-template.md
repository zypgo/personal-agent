# Brief layout

**Answer** (two or three sentences: what the owner should conclude or do)

**Evidence**
- Claim, in one sentence. (Source: name, date, address)
- Claim. (Source: ...)

**Against / caveats**
- The strongest point on the other side, and why it does or does not change the answer.

**Not confirmed**
- What you looked for and could not verify.

**Next step** (only if there is an obvious one)
